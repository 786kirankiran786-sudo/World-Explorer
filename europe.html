About Europe

Europe is a continent located mainly in the Northern Hemisphere. It is known for its historical cities, architecture, museums, education, art and culture. Europe has many countries with different languages and traditions.

Geography of Europe

Europe contains mountains, rivers, plains, forests and beautiful coastlines. The Alps are one of its most famous mountain ranges.

History and Culture

Europe has a long history of ancient civilizations, kingdoms, scientific discoveries and artistic movements. European architecture, literature, music and museums attract visitors from around the world.
