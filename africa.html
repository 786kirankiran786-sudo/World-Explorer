About Africa

Africa is the second-largest continent by both area and population. It is known for its diverse wildlife, ancient history, natural resources, deserts, forests and different cultures.

Geography of Africa

Africa has the Sahara Desert, the Nile River, tropical rainforests, mountains and large grasslands. The continent has many different climate zones.

Wildlife and Nature

Africa is famous for animals such as elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras and rhinos. Its national parks and wildlife reserves are important for nature conservation.

Famous Places

  • Serengeti National Park, Tanzania
  • Victoria Falls
  • Mount Kilimanjaro, Tanzania
  • Sahara Desert
