About Oceania

Oceania is a geographical region that includes Australia, New Zealand, Papua New Guinea and many Pacific island countries. It is known for its beautiful islands, beaches and marine life.

Geography

Oceania contains tropical islands, coral reefs, mountains, forests and deserts. The Pacific Ocean surrounds much of this region.
