🇵🇰 Islamic Republic of Pakistan

Discover Pakistan: Its History, Culture and Beauty

Introduction

Pakistan is a country located in South Asia. It became independent on 14 August 1947. Pakistan is known for its mountains, rivers, historical places, culture and traditions.

Basic Information

Official Name: Islamic Republic of Pakistan

Capital: Islamabad

Largest City: Karachi

Official Language: Urdu

National Language: Urdu

Currency: Pakistani Rupee (PKR)

Independence Day: 14 August 1947

Continent: Asia

National Animal: Markhor

National Bird: Chukar

National Flower: Jasmine

History of Pakistan

Pakistan became an independent country on 14 August 1947. The Pakistan Movement was an important political movement that led to the creation of Pakistan.

Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah played a major role in the creation of Pakistan. Allama Muhammad Iqbal also contributed important ideas to the Pakistan Movement.

Provinces of Pakistan

  • Punjab
  • Sindh
  • Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
  • Balochistan

Islamabad is the federal capital territory. Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir are also important administrative regions.

Geography and Climate

Pakistan has high mountains, deserts, plains, rivers, forests and coastal areas. The Himalayas, Karakoram and Hindu Kush mountain ranges are found in or around northern Pakistan.

Pakistan experiences different types of weather. Summers can be very hot in many areas, while northern mountainous regions can experience cold winters and snowfall.

Culture and Traditions

Pakistani culture includes traditional clothing, music, food, festivals, poetry and hospitality. Shalwar kameez is the national dress. Urdu is widely spoken throughout the country.
