About America

The Americas consist of North America and South America. They include many countries with diverse landscapes, languages, cultures and histories.

North America

North America contains large forests, lakes, mountains, plains and modern cities. It includes countries such as Canada, the United States and Mexico.

South America

South America is famous for the Amazon Rainforest, Andes Mountains, beautiful beaches and rich biodiversity.
