About Asia

Asia is the largest continent in the world by both area and population. It contains many different countries, languages, religions, cultures and traditions. Asia is home to some of the world's oldest civilizations.

Geography of Asia

Asia has mountains, deserts, forests, rivers, islands and beautiful beaches. Mount Everest, the world's highest mountain above sea level, is located in the Himalayas in Asia.
