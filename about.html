About World Explorer

Learn, Explore and Discover the World.

Who We Are

World Explorer is an educational website designed to provide information about countries around the world. Our goal is to make learning about geography, history and cultures simple and interesting.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help students and visitors learn about different countries, their capitals, languages, populations, currencies and traditions.

What You Can Learn

  • Country information
  • World geography
  • Historical information
  • Different cultures
  • Famous tourist places
  • Languages and currencies
